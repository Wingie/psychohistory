## 1. Why the CPU checks were cut

An earlier version of this ledger proposed six CPU-only mechanism checks: quantile balancing on synthetic router logits, subspace quantization on synthetic latents, delta-attention routing contrast on synthetic hidden states, and so on. **All cut as circular.** Implementing an equation and then measuring that the implementation satisfies the equation is not evidence about anything. Load balance on fake logits is decided by the fake logits. Effective dimension on a synthetic latent distribution is decided by the synthetic distribution. None of it constrains behaviour at 2.8T, which is the only question.

Two things survived and neither is a CPU check:

- **The scaling arithmetic** is not a check, it is the paper's Table 1. It is a calculation, it caught three real errors, and it is done.
- **Canary duty-cycle degradation** is not scale-dependent, but the honest version needs a benign null *measured* across two accelerators of different models. That makes it a two-GPU task. It is F8 below.

## 4a. One knob behind three findings: lineage sharing

`../logos.tex` §3.3 is new and it is the largest structural change since the round-2 audit. It names a parameter the paper had been setting to different values in different sections without saying so, and the audit did not catch it **because each of its streams read one section**. This ledger records it here because it reorders §5.

Write `λ` for the degree of **lineage sharing** between towers, on a three-point ladder: `λ = 2` shared corpus (one seed, one continued-pretraining corpus, towers differing only in post-training); `λ = 1` divergent branches (one seed, then asynchronous branches over different corpora, exactly Branch-Train-MiX); `λ = 0` independent (different initialisations, corpora and objectives, no shared basis). High `λ` buys key-value reuse, hidden-state mixing, the Branch-Train-MiX attention-averaging merge and adapter warm start. Low `λ` buys corpus disjointness, alignment isolation and informative difference.

**The conflict is not reconcilable and the paper resolves it against the diversity claim.** Branch-Adapt-Route needs `λ ≥ 1` and there is no version of this architecture without it, so "independently trained towers" weakens to "one common seed, five divergent continued-pretraining branches". Whether a branch is a large enough perturbation to carry the diversity conjecture is not something the paper knows, and it is **falsifier F14**, on the same instrument as F13's limb (a).

**The arithmetic that runs the other way.** With `g` the pre-branch fraction of each tower's token budget, the seed corpus is shared by construction, so `f ≤ 1 - g` and Eq. (residency)'s unique-supply requirement is bounded by

```
5.6e13 x (1 + 4f)  <=  5.6e13 x (5 - 4g)
```

At `g = 0.98` that is **6.05e13**, which is exactly the shared-core threshold §11.4 needs against the central token-supply estimate of about 6e13. At `g = 0` it is the undecomposed **2.8e14**. **The same knob, turned the same way, buys the data ceiling and costs the diversity.** The architecture cannot have both, and the paper had been quietly billing for both.

**Three qualifiers this ledger dropped and the paper carries, restored here.** `../logos.tex` §3.3 is the register of record and its sentence is that a 98 percent common seed "is **one way** to guarantee" the residency fraction the data wall needs.

1. **It is an upper bound, not an equality.** The requirement `5.6e13 (1+4f)` meets a supply of `6e13` exactly when `f <= 0.02`, and `f <= 1-g` is satisfied by `g = 0.20, f = 0.02`. So `g >= 0.98` **suffices and is not needed**, and below it the token argument is undetermined rather than dead. Read the `g` column as a worst case.
2. **The verdict is a function of a point estimate inside a factor-of-ten interval.** With `g* = (5 - S/(r * 2.8e12))/4`, the survey's own 90 percent interval gives `g*` unsatisfiable at any `g` at `S = 2e13`, 0.982 at the central `6e13`, and 0.357 at `S = 2e14`. Nothing in this repository measures `S`.
3. **Correcting `D_opt = 20N` does not rescue it.** At Kimi K2's measured 14.9 and DeepSeek-V3's 22.1 tokens per total parameter, `g*` at the central estimate is 0.890 and 1.008, the second unsatisfiable at any `g`. The input is sound and correcting it hurts the architecture.

The supply premise itself is faithful to its source and is not the weak link: Villalobos et al. give 300T effective stock with a 90 percent interval of 100T to 1000T under a 5x epoch cap, which backs out to `6e13` central with a `2e13` to `2e14` interval, exactly as `../logos.tex:94` states it.

**Three findings in this repository are three views of one parameter, and they should stop being tracked separately:**

| Finding | Where | What it is a view of |
|---|---|---|
| **X-04** (`REVIEW_ROUND2.md`, MEDIUM) | Prop. 2's headroom shrinks by an unbounded amount; `f = 1` takes it to zero | `f`, which is bounded above by `1 - g` |
| **C-02** (`REVIEW_ROUND2.md`, CRITICAL) | The diversity claim is cited to two papers that disclaim it | `λ`; the claim needs `λ = 0`, and `g = 0.98` is `λ = 1` pushed as close to `λ = 2` as it goes |
| **F-04** (`ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md`, HIGH) | Branch-Adapt-Route extrapolated 400× with no hedge | Branch-Adapt-Route is what forces `λ ≥ 1` in the first place |

Fixing any one of them in isolation moves the other two. Raising `g` to rescue the data ceiling (X-04) makes the towers more alike and makes C-02 worse. Dropping Branch-Adapt-Route to rescue the diversity claim (C-02) removes the method F-04 is the extrapolation of, and with it the architecture's update economics. **An earlier revision of this line said "there is no setting of `g` at which all three are satisfied". That is withdrawn**, for the three reasons above: the bound runs one way, the verdict moves across the supply interval, and the token law is not the escape route. **What survives is round 2's X-04 at MEDIUM**, restated in `f` rather than in `g`: a corpus overlap high enough to meet the central supply estimate is a corpus overlap high enough to cost the diversity claim, which `../logos.tex:719` states without `g` at all. §3.3's contribution is an upper bound on `f`; the constraint the diversity claim needs is a *lower* bound on `f`, about which `f <= 1-g` is silent. The honest register of the residue is F14, plus F11 and F12, plus the two new items §4 carries.

**One consequence for `LADDER_ARCHITECTURE.md`, recorded because that document took a decision on the escalated form.** Its §1.5 adopted "response 1", dropping the diversity budget from the architecture's justification, partly on the ground that arithmetic forced a corner. It does not. That document has been revised to re-examine the decision: response 1 still stands on the two legs that survive (the paper's own resolution of L-01, and its §3.4 prediction about the alignment objective), but as a judgement under an unmeasured parameter, and response 2 is no longer excluded by arithmetic.

**Two things §3.3 marks rather than asserts**, and this ledger does not upgrade them: whether Branch-Adapt-Route *strictly* requires a common seed as opposed to being demonstrated on one is a reading of the source, not a quoted claim; and the same question for the Branch-Train-MiX attention-averaging step is a stronger inference, believed sound because averaging weights across unaligned bases is a known failure. Both belong in the prior-art sweep, together with the model-merging literature on permutation alignment, which is where a `λ = 0` merge would have to come from if one exists.

**`PRIOR_ART_v03.md` reaches the same conclusion by a third, independent route.** Its mechanism 4a finds that **KV-cache alignment and ensemble decorrelation are the same knob with opposite signs**: mechanism 1 proposes a training objective that *maximises* representational agreement between ladder members so their KV projections are interchangeable, while 4a wants those same members' errors *decorrelated*, and ensemble benefit exists only insofar as they are. Fort et al. (arXiv:1912.02757) is the load-bearing result: "random initializations explore entirely different modes, while functions along an optimization trajectory or sampled from the subspace thereof cluster within a single mode predictions-wise", and Nam et al. (arXiv:2110.14149) add that "the typical distillation procedure does not effectively transfer such diversity", with the honest scope limit recorded there that Nam et al. study distilling an ensemble into one model, not ensembling students of one teacher. That document's own recommendation is to make it a falsifier: measure sibling error correlation before and after alignment distillation and predict the sign. **So the `g = 0.98` arithmetic, the F14 conjecture, and the mechanism-1-versus-4a tension are three independent arrivals at one statement: commensurability and diversity are bought with the same currency, and this architecture has been spending it twice.**

**Provenance caveat on that document, stated because it bears on how much weight the third route can carry.** `PRIOR_ART_v03.md` §7 lists **thirteen** gaps in its own coverage: six areas not searched at all (non-English literature, patents, closed-lab internal practice, vendor engineering blogs beyond LMSYS, OpenReview submissions under review, code without papers) and seven claims resolved only to a secondary source and therefore not usable as evidence. **The largest single blind spot is non-English literature** (no Chinese-language venues, no `kexue.fm`-class technical blogs, no WeChat or Zhihu posts), which matters because round-2 finding C-06 already turned on a Chinese-language primary source that could not be fetched, and because Moonshot, DeepSeek and Qwen publish substantively in Chinese first. Its mechanism-4a negative is also explicitly weaker than its mechanism-1 negative: "ensemble of distilled siblings" is a phrase with no standard name, so a paper could exist under vocabulary the search did not guess, and that document says in terms not to write "nobody has measured this" without the qualifier.
